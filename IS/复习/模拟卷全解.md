## 1 Q1 安全基础与古典密码

### 1.1 Q1(a) 威胁向量（7 分）

**题面**："Briefly explain 'threat vector' with an example. How a threat vector can be identified?" [7]

**考什么**：**威胁向量**（threat vector）是威胁从哪里来（来源，source）、沿哪条路（路径，path）到达哪个目标（target）。参考答案例子里的**特洛伊木马**（Trojan）是伪装成有用程序、诱人主动运行的恶意代码。建表的完整做法见 [[Tutorial 1 案例精讲#2.1 统一答题框架：威胁向量表与风险登记册|Tutorial 1 案例精讲 2.1]]；"威胁、漏洞、风险"三个词见 [[L1 信息安全基础#3.1 六个常被混用的词|L1 3.1]]。
**今年口径**：今年 L1 正文没讲威胁向量（见 [[L1 信息安全基础#6.3 攻击树|L1 6.3]] 末尾的说明），课件把它放进 tutorial："You build the threat vector table, add likelihood and impact, and produce a ranked risk register with justifications."——**风险登记册**（risk register）是逐行列出风险、评级并给出处置措施的表。今年更可能给一个场景让你建表排序；风险按 L1 的原式 $\text{Risk}=P(\text{threat exploits vulnerability})\times\text{cost of damage}$ 打分。

> [!example] 规范答法（7 分；三条都必写）
> 1. **定义（约 2 分）**：A threat vector describes where a threat originates (its source) and the path it takes to reach a target. It is the route an attack travels, not the attack itself.
> 2. **例子（约 2 分）**：An e-mail sent from outside the organisation to an employee, with an irresistible subject line and an executable attachment that is a Trojan; if opened, it compromises the recipient's computer. Source: an external attacker; path: e-mail → attachment opened by the user; target: the employee's computer and, from there, the internal network.
> 3. **识别方法（约 3 分）**：Create a table listing the threats you are concerned about, with the source, the path and the target of each. To avoid overlooking threat sources, consider all types of threats: external and internal sources, types of attacks, malicious mobile code, advanced persistent threats and manual attacks. Then add likelihood and impact to each row and rank them, which turns the table into a risk register.

> [!warning] 丢分点与参考答案的问题
> - 例子要把"来源、路径、目标"三段写出来。只说"钓鱼邮件"是一个威胁，不是一条向量。
> - 参考答案的识别方法只有一句（"create a table containing a list of threats you are concerned about, along with sources and targets"），对 3 分偏薄：表里没有"路径"一列，可向量恰恰是路径；也没有今年 tutorial 要的可能性和影响。

### 1.2 Q1(b) 病毒与蠕虫（4 分）

**题面**："What is the difference between a virus and a worm?" [4]

**考什么**：今年讲在 [[L10 网络安全：威胁、攻击与防御#2.2 病毒与蠕虫的区别|L10 2.2]]。核心一条：**病毒**（virus）依附于**宿主**（host，被它感染的另一个文件或程序），宿主运行时才跟着运行；**蠕虫**（worm）是自带传播代码的独立程序，主动扫描网络、利用漏洞钻进下一台机器。
**今年口径**：与去年一致，L10 把"是否需要用户动作"作为考场措辞，并提醒按"通常需要 / 不需要"写，不写成绝对。

> [!example] 规范答法（4 分；第 1、2 条必写）
> 1. **依附方式**：A virus attaches itself to a host file or program and replicates by inserting copies of itself into other host code. A worm is a standalone program that carries its own propagation code and does not need to modify other host code to replicate.
> 2. **触发条件**：A virus runs only when its host is executed, so it normally needs human action (opening a file, running a program). A worm usually needs no user action: it actively scans the network and exploits vulnerable services to reach new machines.
> 3. **后果**：Worms therefore tend to spread far faster — scanning worms can spread in minutes (e.g. Slammer, 2003, about 90% of vulnerable hosts in about ten minutes), whereas viruses typically take days or weeks.
> 4. **防御侧重**：Worm propagation is contained by patching and network segmentation; viruses mainly by not running untrusted files and by anti-malware scanning.

> [!warning] 丢分点与参考答案的问题
> - 误："病毒不能自我复制，蠕虫能。" 正：**两者都自我复制**，区别在于要不要宿主、要不要用户动作；不自我复制的是特洛伊木马。
> - "不要用户动作""以分钟计"是典型情况，不是定义：宿主可以由系统自动运行（引导区病毒），群发邮件型蠕虫（2000 年的 ILOVEYOU）要用户打开附件才传播。所以第 2、3 条写 usually / can / typically。
> - 参考答案写到了宿主和"every time the host file is executed, the virus is executed too"，缺的是蠕虫通常不需要用户动作这一对比和传播速度。4 分题只写一点会丢分。

### 1.3 Q1(c) 主动攻击（7 分）

**题面**："Explain active attack with the aid of a diagram. Give some examples of active attacks." [7]

**考什么**：被动 / 主动攻击的分类在 [[L1 信息安全基础#5.2 被动攻击与主动攻击|L1 5.2]]，四类主动攻击在 [[L1 信息安全基础#5.3 具名的攻击|L1 5.3]]，真实网络里的实例在 [[L10 网络安全：威胁、攻击与防御#8.2 四类主动攻击各配本讲的实例|L10 8.2]]。攻击者沿用 Stallings《Cryptography and Network Security》给的名字 Darth。
**今年口径**：今年 L1 按 Stallings 把主动攻击分四类：**冒充**（masquerade）、**重放**（replay）、**篡改**（modification of messages）、**拒绝服务**（denial of service，DoS）。收发方向与 [[L7 公钥密码#3.1 机密性模式：用接收方的公钥加密|L7 3.1]]、L10 统一为 Alice 发给 Bob。

> [!example] 规范答法（7 分；定义、图、四类名称必写，实例可选）
> **定义（约 2 分）**：An active attack involves some modification of the data stream or the creation of a false stream: the attacker (Darth) inserts himself into the communication path between Alice and Bob, so the message that arrives is not the message that was sent. Active attacks threaten integrity, authenticity and availability. Unlike passive attacks they are hard to prevent absolutely, so the goal is to detect them and recover.
>
> **图（约 2 分）**：画 Alice、Bob 和中间的通信设施（Internet or other communications facility），Darth 串在路径上：① Alice → Darth（capture）、② Darth → Bob（insert / alter）、③ Alice → Bob 的正常路径。旁边并排画被动攻击作对比（Darth 只从线上引一条虚线旁听）。每个元素和每条路径都标字。
>
> **例子（约 3 分）**，四类各一句，括号里是图上起作用的路径：
> - **Masquerade** (path ②): one entity pretends to be another — e.g. IP spoofing, or a business email compromise in which an email appears to come from a senior manager.
> - **Replay** (paths ①②③): a valid data unit is captured and retransmitted later — e.g. a stolen session cookie or a captured authentication sequence replayed to gain access.
> - **Modification of messages** (paths ①②): part of a legitimate message is altered, delayed or reordered — e.g. a man in the middle after ARP poisoning altering traffic he relays.
> - **Denial of service** (path ③): normal use of a facility is prevented — e.g. a botnet flooding a web server, or a SYN flood exhausting the half-open connection table.

![[Mock 被动攻击与主动攻击.png]]

*考场版图：(a) 被动，Darth 只旁听；(b) 主动，①②③ 三条路径与四类攻击的对应来自 Stallings 原文。例子里的实例词：IP spoofing 伪造源地址冒充别的主机，business email compromise 冒充高管的诈骗邮件，session cookie 网站记住登录状态的一小段数据，ARP poisoning 骗得局域网流量都经过自己，SYN flood 用大量半开连接塞满服务器，见 [[L10 网络安全：威胁、攻击与防御#8.2 四类主动攻击各配本讲的实例|L10 8.2]]。*

> - 参考答案的例子是 "modification, masquerading, replaying, repudiation, and denial of service"，定义是 "Attacks that threaten the integrity and availability are active attacks"。这套写法来自另一本教材（Forouzan），它按受威胁的安全目标归类，把**抵赖**（repudiation，事后否认自己发过或收过）也算作威胁完整性的主动攻击，参考答案照此列出。今年 L1 用 Stallings 的四类，按四类答；抵赖在今年课件里是 [[L1 信息安全基础#5.4 STRIDE：设计者视角的同一张图|STRIDE]]（微软的威胁建模方法，取六类威胁的英文首字母）里的 R，由不可否认性服务对抗。

### 1.4 Q1(d) Playfair 矩阵与加密（7 分）

**题面**："Design a playfair cipher matrix for a given keyword 'Chengdu'. Encrypt the following plaintext using this playfair cipher matrix: 'My place is secure'" [7]

**考什么**：Playfair 一次把一对字母换成另一对，规则在 [[L2 古典密码与密码分析#6.1 造矩阵|L2 6.1 造矩阵]]、[[L2 古典密码与密码分析#6.2 明文预处理|6.2 预处理]]、[[L2 古典密码与密码分析#6.3 三条加密规则与解密|6.3 三条加密规则]]。
**今年口径**：今年 L2 原话 "The exam will ask you to do both by hand"——加解密和攻击都要会手算，今年除了加密，还可能考解密或说出它的弱点（[[L2 古典密码与密码分析#6.5 它强在哪、为什么还是被破|L2 6.5]]）。

> [!example] 规范答法（7 分；矩阵、分组、规则、逐对结果都必写）
> **Matrix.** Write the keyword CHENGDU (no repeated letters) row by row, then fill the remaining letters in alphabetical order, I and J sharing one cell:
>
> | C | H | E | N | G |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | D | U | A | B | F |
> | I/J | K | L | M | O |
> | P | Q | R | S | T |
> | V | W | X | Y | Z |
>
> - **Pre-processing.** MYPLACEISSECURE → pairs; the repeated pair SS is split by inserting a filler X: MY PL AC EI SX SE CU RE (even length, no final padding needed).
> - **Rules.** Same row → each letter replaced by the letter to its right (wrapping around); same column → the letter below (wrapping around); otherwise → the letter in its own row and the other letter's column.
> - **Result.** MY→SN (same column), PL→RI, AC→DE, EI→CL, SX→RY, SE→RN, CU→HD (rectangles), RE→XA (same column).
> - **Ciphertext: SN RI DE CL RY RN HD XA.**

> [!example]- 逐对手算过程（坐标记作"第几行第几列"，从 0 数起）
>
> | 对 | 位置 | 情形 | 密文 |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | MY | M (2,3)、Y (4,3) | 同列 | M→(3,3)=S，Y→(0,3)=N（绕回顶行）：SN |
> | PL | P (3,0)、L (2,2) | 矩形 | P→(3,2)=R，L→(2,0)=I：RI |
> | AC | A (1,2)、C (0,0) | 矩形 | A→(1,0)=D，C→(0,2)=E：DE |
> | EI | E (0,2)、I (2,0) | 矩形 | E→(0,0)=C，I→(2,2)=L：CL |
> | SX | S (3,3)、X (4,2) | 矩形 | S→(3,2)=R，X→(4,3)=Y：RY |
> | SE | S (3,3)、E (0,2) | 矩形 | S→(3,2)=R，E→(0,3)=N：RN |
> | CU | C (0,0)、U (1,1) | 矩形 | C→(0,1)=H，U→(1,0)=D：HD |
> | RE | R (3,2)、E (0,2) | 同列 | R→(4,2)=X，E→(1,2)=A：XA |
>
> 用解密规则（同行取左、同列取上、矩形不变）逐对还原，得回 MY PL AC EI SX SE CU RE。

> [!warning] 丢分点与参考答案的问题

> - 参考答案的矩阵、分组和密文都对，但只写了分组和结果，没说 SS 为什么插 X、每对用哪条规则。卷面要求 "Show all work"，至少写出三条规则并标出每对的情形。

## 2 Q2 分组密码与公钥密码

### 2.1 Q2(a) AddRoundKey（4 分）

**题面**："Explain the AddRoundKey transformation function for AES method." [4]

**考什么**：AES（Advanced Encryption Standard，高级加密标准）每轮四步之一，见 [[L4 AES#8.1 状态与轮密钥逐字节异或|L4 8.1]]、[[L4 AES#8.2 四步单拿出来都不是密码|L4 8.2]]。**状态**（State）是 128 位分组按列排成的 $4\times4$ 字节矩阵（[[L4 AES#3.1 128 位排成 4×4 字节矩阵，按列填|L4 3.1]]）；**轮密钥**（round key）是由**密钥扩展**（key expansion，[[L4 AES#9.1 4 个字扩成 44 个|L4 9.1]]）得到的每轮一把 128 位密钥；**异或**（XOR）两位相同得 0、不同得 1。
**今年口径**：与去年一致。另外三步是 SubBytes（逐字节查固定代换表）、ShiftRows（按行移位）、MixColumns（每列乘固定矩阵）。**Vernam 密码**是明文与密钥逐位异或的密码；同一把轮密钥加在每个分组上就是密钥重用，所以单独的 AddRoundKey 不是密码。

> [!example] 规范答法（4 分；前两句必写）
> In the forward AddRoundKey transformation, the 128 bits of State are bitwise XORed with the 128 bits of the round key. It can be viewed as a column-wise operation between the 4 bytes of a State column and one word of the round key, or as a byte-level operation. It is the only step that uses the key; it is its own inverse, since $(s \oplus k) \oplus k = s$; and on its own it would be a Vernam cipher with a reused key, so its strength comes from the SubBytes, ShiftRows and MixColumns steps around it.

> [!warning] 丢分点
> 参考答案只写了前两句。4 分题按点给分，"唯一用到密钥""自逆"两点补上更稳。

### 2.2 Q2(b) 雪崩效应（4 分）

**题面**："What is avalanche effect of an encryption algorithm?" [4]

**考什么**：**雪崩效应**（avalanche effect），见 [[L3 分组密码与 DES#7.1 定义与实测|L3 7.1]]：改明文或密钥 1 位，密文约一半的位改变。它是**扩散**（diffusion，把明文统计结构摊到全部密文位上）与**混淆**（confusion，让密钥与密文的关系尽量复杂）做到了没有的可测后果（[[L3 分组密码与 DES#4.2 扩散|L3 4.2]]、[[L3 分组密码与 DES#4.3 混淆|4.3]]）。DES（Data Encryption Standard，数据加密标准，64 位分组）约变 32 位；AES-128 随机试验 2000 次，翻 1 位明文平均变 64.18 位，正是两个随机 128 位串的期望差异 64。
**今年口径**：与去年一致。

> [!example] 规范答法（4 分；第 1、2 条必写）
> 1. The avalanche effect is a desirable property of any encryption algorithm: a small change in either the plaintext or the key should produce a significant change in the ciphertext.
> 2. In particular, changing one bit of the plaintext or one bit of the key should change about half of the ciphertext bits (about 32 of 64 for DES, about 64 of 128 for AES), so the ciphertext looks unrelated to the original one.
> 3. It is the measurable result of good diffusion and confusion.
> 4. Without it, similar plaintexts give similar ciphertexts, and an attacker could adjust guesses step by step towards the answer, turning exhaustive search into a directed search.

> [!warning] 丢分点
> 参考答案只有第 1 条和第 2 条的前半句，没有"约一半"这个量。第 4 条的依据见 [[L3 分组密码与 DES#7.3 没有雪崩会怎样|L3 7.3]]。

### 2.3 Q2(c) ShiftRows（7 分）

**题面**："Explain the process of ShiftRows and perform the ShiftRows operation on the following state matrix." [7]（卷面印作第二个 "(b)"；给出的状态矩阵即答法框表格的"输入"四列）

**考什么**：ShiftRows 是一个**置换**（permutation：不改字节的值，只改位置），第 $r$ 行循环左移 $r$ 个字节（行号从 0 数起），见 [[L4 AES#6.1 第 r 行循环左移 r 个字节|L4 6.1]]；它为什么必要见 [[L4 AES#6.2 没有它，AES 就是四个并排的 32 位密码|L4 6.2]]。这块数据就是 L4 6.1 那张图用的 FIPS 197（定义 AES 的美国联邦标准）附录 B 第 9 轮 SubBytes 之后的状态。

### 2.4 Q2(d) 公钥解密的基本步骤（10 分）

**题面**："What are the essential steps for proper functioning of public key decryption algorithm? Explain with the aid of a diagram." [10]

**今年口径**：这张图是教材图，今年 L7 p7 的 "Confidentiality mode" 一栏讲同一件事；L7 说 "You will be asked to compute with small numbers by hand … it is what the exam asks for"，今年多一道 RSA（[[L7 公钥密码#5.4 完整手算：课堂活动 3|L7 5.4]]）或 Diffie–Hellman（两方在公开信道上约定共同秘密的协议，[[L7 公钥密码#6.3 手算一遍：课堂活动 4|L7 6.3]]）小数手算的可能性很大。

> [!example] 规范答法（10 分：图约 5、步骤约 5；四步与图的五个要素必写，RSA 式子可选）
> Public-key decryption takes the ciphertext and the receiver's private key — the key that matches the public key used for encryption — and produces the original plaintext.
>
> ![[L7 机密性模式（考场英文版）.png]]
>
> Essential steps (Alice sends a confidential message to Bob):
> 1. **Key generation.** Each user generates a pair of mathematically related keys, a public key and a private key. It must be computationally infeasible to derive the private key from the public key.
> 2. **Publication.** Each user places the public key in a public register or other accessible file and keeps the companion private key secret. Each user maintains a collection of public keys obtained from others (a public-key ring).
> 3. **Encryption.** To send a confidential message $X$ to Bob, Alice takes Bob's public key $PU_b$ from her ring and computes $Y=\mathrm{E}(PU_b,X)$. With RSA, $PU_b=\{e,n\}$ and $Y=X^e \bmod n$.
> 4. **Decryption.** Bob decrypts with his own private key: $X=\mathrm{D}(PR_b,Y)$. With RSA, $PR_b=\{d,n\}$ and $X=Y^d\bmod n$, which recovers $X$ because $ed\equiv1 \pmod{\phi(n)}$. No other recipient can decrypt, because only Bob knows $PR_b$; it must also be computationally infeasible to recover $X$ from $PU_b$ and $Y$.

> - 加密用**接收方**的公钥，解密用**接收方**的私钥。参考答案（教材原图）是 Bob 发给 Alice、写 $PU_a$、$PR_a$，本篇按 L7 写 Alice 发给 Bob，两种都对；丢分的是混用。

### 3.3 Q3(a)(iii) 三类链的准入（3 分）

**题面**："Explain the access permission respectively for public chain, private chain and consortium chain." [3 marks]

**考什么**：[[L12 区块链系统概览#2.5 三类对照与规范答法|L12 2.5]]（课件 p21–p24）。**无许可**（permissionless）谁都能加入和记账，**许可**（permissioned）要经批准；联盟链与私有链的区别只在许可由谁发。

### 3.4 Q3(b)(i) 两类故障（4 分）

**题面**："List and explain two typical failures occurred in a blockchain system." [4 marks]

**考什么**：[[L15 区块链共识 I#2.2 两类节点，两类故障|L15 2.2]]。**共识**（consensus）是让一组节点对同一件事（下一块是什么）得出相同结论，协议要在节点出故障时照常工作。**崩溃故障**（crash failure）是节点停机、不收不发、不说假话；**拜占庭故障**（Byzantine failure）是节点可以任意行为、可以说谎，名字来自 Leslie Lamport（分布式系统学者）等人 1982 年的"拜占庭将军问题"。

> [!example] 规范答法（4 分：名称各 1、解释各 1）
> - **Crash failure**: the node cannot work properly (either receiving data or transmitting data), e.g. it crashes or goes offline.
> - **Byzantine failure**: the node can arbitrarily send any false information (e.g. conflicting messages to different nodes), typically because it is malicious or compromised.

> [!warning] 按行为分，不按身份分
> 课件 p23 字面按"合法节点 / 恶意节点"区分；标准定义按出故障时的**行为**区分，诚实节点的软件出 bug、乱发消息也算拜占庭故障。参考答案按行为写，比课件准确，照参考答案的口径答。

### 3.5 Q3(b)(ii) 共识机制（3 分）

**题面**："List any three consensus mechanisms used in blockchain systems." [3 marks]

**考什么**：[[L15 区块链共识 I#2.4 常见共识机制一览|L15 2.4]]，课件路线图上的五个名字：
- **工作量证明**（Proof of Work，PoW）、**权益证明**（Proof of Stake，PoS）：公有链按算力或质押的币"抽签"决定谁记账；
- **Paxos**（Lamport 提出的经典协议）、**RAFT**：许可链按多数派投票，只防崩溃故障；RAFT 先选出一个 **leader**（领导者节点），再由它把**日志**（log，按顺序记下的命令列表）复制给大家；
- **实用拜占庭容错**（Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance，PBFT）：多轮投票，能防拜占庭故障，需 $3f+1$ 个节点（$f$ 是要容忍的故障节点数）。

> [!example] 规范答法（3 分，每个 1 分）
> Proof of Work (PoW), Proof of Stake (PoS), Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT).（任选三个，也可写 Paxos、RAFT。参考答案 "PoW, PoS, RAFT, Paxos, PBFT, etc." 与课件一致；题目是 "List"，写全称即可。）

### 3.6 Q3(b)(iii) 吞吐量与确认时间（3 分）

**题面**："Suppose a blockchain network has a block size of 6 MBytes, transaction size of 80 Bytes, Block time is 200 seconds and it requires to wait for 20 blocks to confirm any new block. Calculate the average block confirmation time and transaction per second (throughput) of this blockchain network." [3 marks]

**考什么**：[[L15 区块链共识 I#5.2 吞吐量：每块装几笔，每块用几秒|L15 5.2]] 的**交易吞吐量**（transaction throughput，每秒处理的交易数，单位 Tx/s）= 区块大小 ÷（交易大小 × **出块时间**）；[[L15 区块链共识 I#5.4 确认时间：要等几块|L15 5.4]] 的**区块确认时间**（block confirmation time）= 需等待的块数 × 出块时间，等的块越多，新块被推翻的概率越小，小到可以接受才算确认。课程按 1 MB = $10^6$ B 算，作答时把这一步写出来。

> [!example] 规范答法（3 分：代入公式 1、吞吐量结果 1、确认时间 1；全部必写）
> Taking 1 MB = $10^6$ bytes:
>
> $$
> \text{Transaction Throughput}=\frac{\text{Block Size}}{\text{Transaction Size}\times\text{Block Time}}=\frac{6\times10^6\ \text{Bytes}}{80\ \text{Bytes}\times200\ \text{s}}\tag{1}
> $$
>
> $$
> \text{Transaction Throughput}=\frac{6\times10^6}{1.6\times10^4}=375\ \text{Tx/s}\tag{2}
> $$
>
> $$
> \text{Average Confirmation Time}=20\times200\ \text{s}=4000\ \text{s}\tag{3}
> $$
>
> 验算：每块 $6\times10^6\div80=75\,000$ 笔，每 200 s 一块，$75\,000\div200=375$ Tx/s；4000 s 约 66.7 分钟。

> [!warning] 丢分点与参考答案的问题
> - **参考答案的 37.5 Tx/s 错了 10 倍**，正确是 375 Tx/s。按 1 MB = $2^{20}$ B（严格说那是 MiB）算是 393.216 Tx/s，两种口径都得不到 37.5；37.5 要求区块只有 $37.5\times80\times200=600\,000$ B。今年 L15 课件例题（2 MB、500 B、200 s）写 200 Tx/s，实为 20 Tx/s，方向相反地错了 10 倍，见 [[L15 区块链共识 I#5.6 例题：课件 Q2 与模拟卷原题|L15 5.6]]。
> - 吞吐量的分母只用出块时间 200 s；确认时间 4000 s 只说明多久算确认，不进吞吐量公式。课件 p49–p51 把两者混写，照着把 4000 代进去会算成 18.75 Tx/s，见 [[L15 区块链共识 I#5.5 出块时间与确认时间别混|L15 5.5]]。

### 3.7 Q3(b)(iv) 崩溃容错的节点数（3 分）

**题面**："If there are 3 crash-failure nodes participating in a distributed consensus process, calculate the minimum number of legitimate nodes to achieve crash-failure tolerance (the derivation should be provided)." [3 marks]

**考什么**：[[L16 区块链共识 II#3.2 崩溃故障：N ≥ 2f+1|L16 3.2]]、[[L16 区块链共识 II#3.5 考场写法|3.5]]，课件 p38 的 Note："to tolerate f faulty acceptors, at least a set consisting of 2f + 1 acceptors is required."。推导靠**法定人数**（quorum：每一步必须等到的回应个数）的交集。

> [!example] 规范答法（3 分：推导与不等式 2、减法 1；四行都写）
> 1. Let $N$ be the total number of nodes and $f$ the number of faulty nodes. Since $f$ nodes may never respond, each step can wait for at most $N-f$ replies (quorum $q=N-f$).
> 2. Any two quorums overlap in at least $2(N-f)-N=N-2f$ nodes. Crashed nodes do not lie, so a non-empty overlap is enough: $N-2f\ge1$, i.e. $N\ge2f+1$.
> 3. With $f=3$: $N\ge2\times3+1=7$.
> 4. Minimum number of legitimate nodes $=7-3=4$.

![[Mock 法定人数交集 f=3.png]]

*上：崩溃容错，7 个节点里任意两组 4 个必有 1 个公共节点。下：拜占庭容错（[[#4.4 Q4(a)(iv) 拜占庭容错的节点数（3 分）|Q4(a)(iv)]]），10 个节点里任意两组 7 个有 4 个公共节点，扣掉至多 3 个作恶的仍剩 1 个诚实节点。*

> [!warning] 丢分点与参考答案的问题

> - 写成 $N>2f+1$；正确是 $N\ge2f+1$。
> - 参考答案只给了一行 $N\ge2\times3+1=7$，题面却要求 "the derivation should be provided"，照规范答法写出法定人数与交集两步。

### 3.8 Q3(b)(v) 分片的优点（2 分）

**题面**："Explain the advantages of sharing." [2 marks]（"sharing" 是 "sharding" 的笔误）

**考什么**：[[L14 区块链工作原理（下）#6.5 分片|L14 6.5]]。**分片**（sharding）把节点分成若干组，各组并行处理一部分交易，吞吐量随分片数增长。

> [!example] 规范答法（2 分，任两点各 1；前两条有课件依据，优先写）
> Sharding divides the nodes into groups (shards); each shard processes its own share of transactions in parallel. Advantages:
> - **High scalability**: adding nodes allows more shards, so throughput grows with the size of the network instead of staying fixed.
> - **Greater transaction capacity**: shards process transactions in parallel, so the total throughput is roughly the sum over all shards, removing the transaction-capacity bottleneck.
> - **Low cost**: each node only processes and stores the transactions of its own shard, which reduces its computation, storage and bandwidth cost.

> [!warning] 参考答案的问题
> 题面 "sharing" 应为 "sharding"，答题第一句写出 "Sharding divides…" 表明按分片作答。参考答案的 "Low cost" 课件里没有，写它时附上"每个节点只处理本分片"这句理由。

## 4 Q4 共识与区块链应用

### 4.1 Q4(a)(i) PoW 获胜概率（3 分）

**题面**："Consider a PoW-based blockchain system with five participating nodes (N1, N2, N3, N4), with computing power (hash rate) values ϕ1=3, ϕ2=5, ϕ3=4, and ϕ4=x. If the probability of winning the puzzle by N1 is 20%, calculate the value of x." [3 marks]

**考什么**：PoW（[[#3.5 Q3(b)(ii) 共识机制（3 分）|3.5]]）里节点获胜概率等于它的**算力**（hash rate，每秒能算的哈希次数）占全网的比例，$P_{w_i}=\varphi_i/\sum_j\varphi_j$，推导见 [[L15 区块链共识 I#3.2 获胜概率：算力占比|L15 3.2]]，例题见 [[L15 区块链共识 I#3.3 例题：求获胜概率与反求算力|L15 3.3]]（课件正文写作 ϕ，与 $\varphi$ 是同一个量）。通法：先由"份额 = 自己 ÷ 总量"求总量，再减已知部分。

> [!example] 规范答法（3 分，三行各 1；全部必写）
>
> $$
> P_{w_1}=\frac{\varphi_1}{\varphi_1+\varphi_2+\varphi_3+\varphi_4}=20\%\tag{4}
> $$
>
> $$
> \varphi_1+\varphi_2+\varphi_3+\varphi_4=\frac{\varphi_1}{0.2}=\frac{3}{0.2}=15\tag{5}
> $$
>
> $$
> x=\varphi_4=15-(3+5+4)=3\tag{6}
> $$
>
> Check: $3/(3+5+4+3)=3/15=20\%$.

> [!warning] 参考答案的问题
> 题面写 "five participating nodes"，括号里只列了 N1–N4 四个、算力也只给了四个，是卷面笔误，按四个节点解。考场上可加一句 "assuming the four listed nodes are all the participants"。

### 4.2 Q4(a)(ii) PoS 相对 PoW 的优点（2 分）

**题面**："Discuss the advantages of PoS over PoW." [2 marks]

### 4.3 Q4(a)(iii) Paxos 的节点与阶段（5 分）

**题面**："Paxos is a fundamental consensus mechanism in distributed systems to reach an agreement by using a broadcast way. Explain the main process of Paxos, including the nodes and phases." [5 marks]

**考什么**：三种角色是**提案者**（proposer，提出值）、**接受者**（acceptor，投票）、**学习者**（learner，得知结果），规范答法见 [[L16 区块链共识 II#4.9 考场答法：Paxos 的节点与阶段|L16 4.9]]；角色与阶段的讲解在 [[L16 区块链共识 II#4.2 三种角色|L16 4.2]]、[[L16 区块链共识 II#4.4 Phase 1：prepare|4.4]]、[[L16 区块链共识 II#4.5 Phase 2：accept|4.5]]，每条规则为什么必要见 [[L16 区块链共识 II#4.7 每条规则为什么必要：两个提案者的例子|4.7]]；acceptor 细则以 L16 为准（[[L17 区块链共识 III#2.1 相对 L16 课件的六处改动|L17 的回顾]]删掉了它们）。安全的关键：新旧两个多数派必相交，交集里的 acceptor 报告已接受的最高编号提案，新 proposer 只能沿用那个值。

> [!example] 规范答法（5 分，每条约 1；时间紧时每条只写第一句）
> 1. **Setting and idea.** Paxos reaches consensus on a single value over an unreliable network: messages may be delayed, lost, out of order or duplicated, but not corrupted; nodes may crash and restart but are not Byzantine. A majority represents the whole: any two majorities intersect, so to tolerate $f$ faulty acceptors, $2f+1$ acceptors are needed.
> 2. **Nodes (roles).** Proposer: receives client requests and proposes values; usually one elected proposer acts as the leader. Acceptor: promises / accepts or rejects proposals according to the rules. Learner: learns the decided value. One process may play all three roles.
> 3. **Phase 1 (prepare).** The proposer chooses a globally unique proposal number $n$, larger than any it has used, and sends prepare($n$) to a majority (or all) of the acceptors. If $n$ is greater than any number the acceptor has promised, it promises to ignore proposals numbered less than $n$, records $n$ and replies promise($n$), attaching the highest-numbered proposal $(n',v')$ it has accepted, if any; otherwise it ignores the request. If it times out, the proposer retries with a higher $n$.
> 4. **Phase 2 (accept).** After receiving promises from a majority ($f+1$ of the $2f+1$ acceptors), the proposer picks $v$: the value of the highest-numbered proposal reported in the promises, or any value (e.g. the client's) if none was reported. It sends accept($n$, $v$). An acceptor accepts unless it has promised a higher number, raises its promised number to $n$ (normally it is one that replied promise($n$)), and replies accepted($n$, $v$).
> 5. **Decision and learning.** Once a majority of acceptors have accepted $(n,v)$, $v$ is decided. Each acceptor sends accepted($n$, $v$) to all learners; learning is part of phase 2 (drawn as a separate learn phase only for visualisation).
>

### 4.4 Q4(a)(iv) 拜占庭容错的节点数（3 分）

**题面**："If there are 3 Byzantine attackers participating in a distributed consensus process, calculate the minimum number of legitimate nodes to achieve Byzantine attack tolerance (derivations should be provided)." [3 marks]

**考什么**：[[L16 区块链共识 II#3.3 拜占庭故障：N ≥ 3f+1|L16 3.3]]、[[L16 区块链共识 II#3.5 考场写法|3.5]]，课件 p67 PBFT 的 $n\ge3f+1$。推导与 [[#3.7 Q3(b)(iv) 崩溃容错的节点数（3 分）|Q3(b)(iv)]] 同一套，多了"回应里有人说谎"这一条；图见 3.7 的下半。只有 $N=3f=9$ 个节点时，两组法定人数（各 6 个）交集 3 个，可能全是作恶节点，所以不够。理解用、非作答：$N=10$ 的 PBFT 一轮，不含客户端的 request、含 reply 共 $9+81+90+10=190$ 条消息，不计 reply 为 $2N(N-1)=180$ 条，见 [[L16 区块链共识 II#6.7 代价：O(N²) 条消息|L16 6.7]]。

> [!example] 规范答法（3 分：推导与不等式 2、减法 1；四行都写）
> 1. Each step can wait for at most $N-f$ replies, because $f$ nodes may not respond.
> 2. Among these $N-f$ replies, up to $f$ may come from Byzantine nodes (the silent ones may be honest but slow), so honest replies $\ge N-2f$. Honest replies must outnumber faulty ones: $N-2f>f$, i.e. $N\ge3f+1$. (Equivalently: two quorums of $N-f$ overlap in $N-2f$ nodes, and at least one of them must be honest: $N-2f\ge f+1$.)
> 3. With $f=3$: $N\ge3\times3+1=10$.
> 4. Minimum number of legitimate nodes $=10-3=7$.

> [!warning] 丢分点与参考答案的问题
> - 答成总数 10；把拜占庭模型的法定人数写成"多数"（$f+1$），多数只够防崩溃，拜占庭要 $2f+1$（这里是 7）。
> - 参考答案同样只有一行不等式，缺推导。

### 4.5 Q4(a)(v) RAFT 的角色与拜占庭（4 分）

**题面**："List the three roles that the nodes can take in the RAFT consensus mechanism and indicate if the RAFT can resist Byzantine attacks/failures." [4 marks]

**考什么**：[[L16 区块链共识 II#5.2 三种角色与任期|L16 5.2]]、[[L16 区块链共识 II#5.6 RAFT 不能抵抗拜占庭故障|L16 5.6]]；复习页见 [[L18 区块链赋能的认知无线电网络#1.1 RAFT：容崩溃，不容拜占庭|L18 1.1]]。RAFT 里 leader 接收请求、用 **AppendEntries**（leader 向 follower 追加日志的请求）复制日志，并定期发**心跳**（heartbeat，不带日志条目的 AppendEntries，表示"leader 还在"）；follower 被动跟随；follower 在**选举超时**（election timeout，一段时间收不到心跳）后变成 candidate，把**任期**（term，选举轮次编号）加 1 去拉票，得多数票当选。提交条件：leader 把**本任期**创建的一条日志复制到多数节点（leader 自己算一份），它就算提交，更早任期留下的条目随之一并提交，见 [[L16 区块链共识 II#5.5 为什么按本任期多数提交就安全（课件外）|L16 5.5]]。

> [!example] 规范答法（4 分，每项 1；名字与 "cannot" 必写，理由一句可选）
> **Leader**, **Follower** and **Candidate**. **RAFT cannot resist Byzantine attacks/failures.**
>
> Followers accept the leader's AppendEntries without verification (no signatures, no cross-checking), so a malicious leader could send different logs to different followers; RAFT only tolerates crash failures ($N\ge2f+1$).

> [!warning] 丢分点
> 理由别写成"作恶节点报一个大任期号就能当选"：任期号报大只会逼别人退回 follower、反复打断选举；要真当选还得谎报自己的日志足够新骗票。RAFT 不验证这些自报内容，才是它防不住拜占庭节点的原因。

### 4.6 Q4(c)(i) 避免接入碰撞的好处（2 分）

Q4(c) 题干："Applying blockchain to wireless cognitive radio is promising to establish a trusted spectrum sharing scheme." **认知无线电**（cognitive radio，CR）是让设备感知哪些频段空着、动态借用的技术（[[L18 区块链赋能的认知无线电网络#3.3 认知无线电：会自己找空频段的电台|L18 3.3]]）。四小问全部出自 L18。

**题面**："List the any two benefits of avoiding user access collisions happening in a wireless communication network." [2 marks]

**考什么**：[[L18 区块链赋能的认知无线电网络#6.3 为什么必须避免碰撞|L18 6.3]]，课件 p37："otherwise, packets will be lost, re-transmission will be required and thus result in energy, spectrum and time consumption."——**碰撞**（collision）是两个用户同时在同一信道发射、互相干扰。

> [!example] 规范答法（2 分；写三条）
> 1. **Reduce energy consumption**: collisions cause packet loss and retransmission; avoiding them saves the energy of retransmitting.
> 2. **Improve spectrum utilization**: a collided transmission occupies the channel without delivering anything; without collisions every use of the spectrum carries useful data.
> 3. **Reduce latency**: no waiting for backoff and retransmission.

> [!warning] 参考答案的问题
> 题面要两条、2 分，参考答案却写 "Accept any three of the below, and 1 mark each"，前后不一致；参考答案的 "Improve system throughout" 是 throughput 的笔误。按 2 分题写三条最稳。参考答案另两条 improve throughput、improve connectivity 也给分，后者课件没提。

### 4.7 Q4(c)(ii) 授权与非授权频谱（2 分）

**题面**："Explain licensed spectrum and unlicensed spectrum in a wireless radio system." [2 marks]

**考什么**：[[L18 区块链赋能的认知无线电网络#2.3 授权频谱与非授权频谱|L18 2.3]]、[[L18 区块链赋能的认知无线电网络#5.1 主用户与次用户|5.1]]。**授权频谱**（licensed spectrum）由政府管理、须购买许可，持证用户叫**主用户**（primary user，PU）；CR 节点是**次用户**（secondary user，SU），只能在主用户空闲时借用。**非授权频谱**（unlicensed spectrum）人人免费可用，典型是 **ISM 频段**（Industrial, Scientific and Medical，工业、科学、医疗频段，Wi-Fi 的 2.4 GHz 在其中）。

> [!example] 规范答法（2 分，每类 1；每类第一句必写）
> - **Licensed spectrum** is managed by the government, and only licensed users have the right to use it (e.g., telecom operators who buy the band, i.e., the primary users); it is protected from other transmitters. In a cognitive radio network, secondary users may use it opportunistically only when the primary user is idle, without causing harmful interference, and must leave when the primary user returns.
> - **Unlicensed spectrum** can be used free of charge by everyone without a licence, e.g., the ISM band used by Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and ZigBee; no user has exclusive rights or protection from interference.

> [!warning] 补充
> 参考答案 "licensed spectrum can only be used by licensed users [1 mark], unlicensed spectrum can be used by everyone in the system [1 mark]" 与课件一致；补上次用户借用一句是加分写法，因为这道题挂在认知无线电题干下。ISM 只是非授权频谱的典型例子，二者不等同，所以写 "e.g."。
